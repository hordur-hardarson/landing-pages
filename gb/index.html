03

House Systems & Amenities

Ice Machine

Located in the laundry room. Available throughout your stay.

  1. Add fresh water to the reservoir
  2. Turn the machine ON
  3. Ice is ready in approximately 10 minutes

To empty: remove the plug in the front right corner underneath — drain into the sink. No need to turn off at checkout.

Sauna — Harvia Kip Electric Oven
Left Dial
Timer

Turn clockwise past first section. Set 1–4 hours for immediate heat.

Right Dial
Temperature

Clockwise = hotter  ·  Counter-clockwise = cooler

⚠️
Delay feature: If the timer dial is turned too far into the 1–8 hour delay section, the sauna will not heat immediately. Turn it back to the 1–4 hour range for instant start.
  • Sauna turns off automatically when the timer runs out
  • Always double-check it is OFF before leaving the house
Hot Tub
ℹ️
The control panel is in the storage room inside the guest house — at the opposite end from the entrance.

Controls

  • One control turns the water ON / OFF
  • One control opens / closes the drain at the bottom
  • Do not adjust any internal technical settings

Filling

  1. Turn ON the water
  2. Close the drain (turn both buttons)
  3. Allow the tub to fill fully — temperature maintained automatically
  4. Always place the lid on between uses

Emptying — Required Before Departure

⚠️
Mandatory: The hot tub must be emptied before you leave.
  1. Turn OFF the water
  2. Open the drain
  3. Allow to empty completely (no need to monitor)
  4. Brush the sides of the tub while half-empty

Lid Instructions

The lid is heavy — two people recommended. The patio can be very slippery when wet.

Groceries, Liquor & ATMs

🛒
There are no grocery stores, liquor stores, or ATMs in Húsafell. Plan all shopping before arriving, or stop on the way.
Nearest Town — Borgarnes

Approximately a 50 minute drive from the house.

Liquor Store
  • Nearest Vínbúðin: Borgarnes — limited opening hours, plan ahead
  • Order online with pickup at smarikid.is — open daily except Sundays until 23:00
Cards & ATMs
  • Credit and debit cards are universally accepted in Iceland
  • Very little need for cash — even parking, fuel, and cafés accept cards
  • ATMs available in Borgarnes — none in Húsafell
