Beyond Passport

There's More Than One Way to Enjoy Iceland

Many of our guests return to Iceland year after year. Some eventually begin dreaming about owning a holiday home here.

Most people assume buying an entire property is the only option. It isn't.

Heimkoma also helps families enjoy premium holiday homes through professionally managed shared ownership. Instead of owning an entire home, you can own a share in one carefully selected holiday home — or even a portfolio of homes located in different parts of Iceland.

We take care of everything, including furnishing, maintenance, bookings and day-to-day management, so you simply enjoy your time in Iceland.

If you'd ever like to learn more, we'd be happy to tell you how it works.
