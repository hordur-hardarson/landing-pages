House Systems & Amenities

Ice Machine

The ice machine is built into the fridge. It does not require a separate unit.

  1. Open the fridge and locate the small water tank inside
  2. Fill the tank with fresh water
  3. Ice will form in the icebox in the lower half of the fridge
Hot Tub
🔑
A separate lockbox is located next to the hot tub. It uses the same code as the main entrance lockbox. Inside you will find the key for the hot tub switch.

Hot tub lockbox — contains the key used to turn on the hot tub. The code is the same as the code for the house-key lockbox.

ℹ️
Before using: remove the tarp and the metal cross that cover the hot tub. Set them aside and return them before leaving.

Using the Hot Tub

  1. Use the key from the lockbox to unlock the switch
  2. Turn the switch ON — the hot tub will begin filling
  3. Keep the switch ON while using the hot tub
  4. When finished, turn the switch OFF — the hot tub empties automatically
  5. Remove the key and return it to the lockbox
⚠️
Before leaving: make sure the switch is OFF, the tub has emptied, and the tarp and metal cross are back in place.
Cold Tub
❄️
The cold tub is not available in winter. It operates in summer only.

Using the Cold Tub

  1. Remove the wooden cover and set it aside
  2. Insert the cork into the drain at the bottom
  3. Turn the knob to start the cold water
  4. When finished, turn off the water
  5. Remove the cork — the tub will drain
  6. Replace the wooden cover

06

Activities & Attractions

Akureyri is Iceland's second largest city and the capital of the north. Most attractions are within walking distance or a short drive.

In Town
  • Akureyri Swimming Pool (Sundlaug) — one of Iceland's finest public pools, with hot tubs and waterslides
  • Akureyri Botanical Garden (Lystigarður) — northernmost botanical garden in the world, free entry
  • Akureyri Church (Akureyrarkirkja) — landmark building with views over the fjord
  • Restaurants and bars — concentrated in the pedestrianised center, a short walk from the house
Winter Activities
  • Hlíðarfjall ski resort — Iceland's largest ski area, 10 minutes from town. Downhill and cross-country skiing.
  • Northern lights viewing — Akureyri's northern latitude makes it one of the best places in Iceland to see the aurora
  • Snowmobiling and dog sledding — available in the surrounding area
Summer Activities
  • Whale watching — Eye'jaörður fjord is one of the best locations in Iceland for whale watching. Tours depart from Akureyri harbour.
  • Midnight sun hiking — June and July offer near-24-hour daylight
  • Horse riding — Icelandic horse tours available nearby
  • Fishing — river and sea fishing in the surrounding area
Day Trips from Akureyri
  • Lake Mývatn — 1 hour east. Geothermal landscape, pseudo-craters, nature baths, and bird life.
  • Goðafoss waterfall — 45 minutes east. One of Iceland's most photographed waterfalls.
  • Dettifoss — Europe's most powerful waterfall, approximately 2 hours east.
  • Siglufjörður — 1 hour west. Charming fishing village with the Herring Era Museum.
  • Tröllaskagi peninsula — dramatic mountain and fjord scenery, skiing in winter.

Further information and bookings: visitakureyri.is
